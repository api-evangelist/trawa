# trawa (Future Energy Services GmbH)

> trawa is a Berlin-based energy management company for mid-sized businesses. It combines AI-optimized green-electricity procurement on the wholesale market with cloud energy-management software that gives customers real-time visibility into consumption, costs, and CO2 emissions, plus battery storage and smart control of energy-consuming assets. Founded in 2022; backed by Balderton Capital and Speedinvest. trawa exposes a customer web portal and a trust center but publishes no public developer API at this time.
